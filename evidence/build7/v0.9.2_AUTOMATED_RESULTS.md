# VoiceStrike BUILD 7 v0.9.2 — Automated Evidence

## Root cause fixed

Live v0.9.1 telemetry showed `reverse_last_scan` blocked locally with
`CRITICAL_SPEECH_NOT_TRUSTED` after a successful authoritative inspect. The protected worker phrase
was judged against a global speech-start + provider `reply.done` quiet timer. Provider completion
does not prove local PCM has finished, and the fixed 1.5 s delay could also reject a normal worker
who answered immediately after the audible prompt.

v0.9.2 introduces an explicit protected speech window bound to epoch + command + action + component
+ expected critical kind and opens it from estimated audible PCM completion.
